03 / BEFORE YOU JOIN

A little clarity.

Know what to expect.

What is AXIOM?

AXIOM is a Telegram trading community sharing trade ideas and educational perspectives. It is a place to explore the markets and develop your own thinking.

How do I join?

Select any “Join AXIOM” button to open our Telegram invite. You can open it in the Telegram app or through Telegram’s website and follow the group joining prompts.

Are the trade ideas financial advice?

No. Content reflects personal trade ideas and general education. It is not personalised investment advice or a recommendation suited to your circumstances. You are responsible for your own decisions.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Trading involves risk, including the loss of your capital. Leveraged products can lead to rapid losses. Past performance does not indicate future results, and no trade idea guarantees a profit.

Does AXIOM receive referral commission?

Built around our community. AXIOM earns commission from partner brokers when clients referred through our links trade. We reinvest 60% of the broker commission we receive into community education, research tools and resources.
